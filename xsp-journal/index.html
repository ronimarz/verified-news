Break, then hold 60 seconds

The app checks finished 1-minute candles. A candle that closes past your level is the break. The next full candle that never touches the level again is the 60-second hold. If price comes back across, the count starts over.

Tap when a candle closes beyond the level. If price comes back across, tap Reset and the clock starts over.

Test the rule on past days

This looks back at real SPY days and asks one question: when SPY broke a key level, was it better to buy right away, wait 60 seconds, or wait 3 minutes?

  1. Press Run the test. You don't need to change anything.
  2. Wait about 20 seconds while it loads the past days.
  3. Read the answer at the top. The rule with the green Best tag did best.
Change settings (optional)

Defaults: 20 days, win at $0.75, loss at $0.50. If you change these, press Run the test again.
