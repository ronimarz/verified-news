Break, then hold

The app checks finished 1-minute candles. A candle that closes past your level is the break. If today's strategy waits for a hold, each following full candle that never touches the level again counts as one minute of hold. If price comes back across, the count starts over.

Tap when a candle closes beyond the level. If price comes back across, tap Reset and the clock starts over.

Looks through SPY's past trading days to find what tends to happen, which days looked like today, and which rules would have worked. These are tendencies, not promises.
